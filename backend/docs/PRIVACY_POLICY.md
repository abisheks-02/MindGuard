# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2026

MindGuard is committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** of India.

## 1. What Data We Collect
- **Anonymous Mode**: We collect text inputs tied to a random `session_id`. We do not ask for your name or phone number.
- **Authenticated Mode**: We collect your phone number, strictly encrypted at-rest using AES-128-CBC.

## 2. Purpose Limitation
Your text inputs are used **exclusively** to provide emotional support and risk detection. They are processed locally on our servers using Llama 3.2. **No text data is ever sent to third-party APIs like OpenAI or Anthropic.**

## 3. Data Retention
- Anonymous logs are automatically purged after 90 days of inactivity.
- You have the absolute right to erasure.

## 4. Your Rights under DPDP Act
You have the right to request access, correction, and **permanent erasure** of your data. Use the `Delete My Data` option in Settings to execute a hard cascade deletion of all your records.
